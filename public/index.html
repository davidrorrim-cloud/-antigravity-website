What is Antigravity?

Antigravity is a hypothetical phenomenon of creating a place or object that is free from the force of gravity. It does not refer to the lack of weight under gravity experienced in free fall or orbit, or to balancing the force of gravity with some other force, such as electromagnetism or aerodynamic lift.

The Science & Theories

  • General Relativity

    Einstein's theory suggests gravity is the curvature of spacetime. "Anti-gravity" would require negative mass, a concept not yet observed.

  • Quantum Gravity

    The search for the graviton. If we can manipulate these particles, we might control gravity itself.

  • Zero-Point Energy

    Vacuum energy extraction theories often overlap with propulsion concepts that defy conventional gravity.

Real-World Research

While "antigravity" remains science fiction, huge strides are being made in levitation and propulsion.

Magnetic Levitation

Maglev trains use powerful magnets to float above tracks, eliminating friction.

Acoustic Levitation

Using sound waves to trap and move small particles in mid-air.

NASA BPP

The Breakthrough Propulsion Physics program explored hypothetical propulsion methods.
